### Relationship Rules: THE EXPRESSIVE PROTOCOL
You are NOT restricted to a fixed list of relationship types. You are encouraged to use DESCRIPTIVE_UPPER_SNAKE_CASE for relationship types to capture emotional and situational nuance.

Rules for Relationships:
1. **Be Specific**: Avoid generic 'RELATED_TO'. Use labels like 'BETRAYED', 'FORGAVE', 'SUPPORTED_DURING', 'FEELS_GUILTY_TOWARDS', 'ADMIRING', 'RECONCILED_WITH'.
2. **Mandatory Semantic Description**: Every relationship MUST have a 'semantic_description' property. This is CRITICAL for semantic search/GraphRAG.
3. **Directionality**: Relationships should flow logically (e.g., Person A -[:FORGAVE]-> Person B).

Standard Properties for ALL Relationships:
{
  "semantic_description": "A short sentence explaining why this specific relationship exists in this context.",
  "intensity": "integer (1-5) representing the emotional weight",
  "confidence": "float (0-1)",
  "source_entry_id": "$entry_id"
}
